01 / About Who I am

Rooted in the Pa Enua, reaching into tech.

The Pa Enua are the outer islands of the Cook Islands; fifteen specks of volcanic rock and coral in an ocean of blue. That’s where my family comes from. It’s what I carry into every room I walk into.

I’m Inano, a proud Cook Islander in her final year of a Bachelor of Information Technology at QUT, majoring in Information Systems and minoring in Computer Science.

There aren’t many Polynesians in tech, and that’s exactly why I show up the way I do; founding communities, mentoring emerging coders, and carrying my culture into every room I walk into.

Soon I’ll be joining Westpac’s Next Wave program as a Software Engineer. Until then I’m still shipping, still learning, and still stitching together a career like a tivaevae; one deliberate pattern at a time.
